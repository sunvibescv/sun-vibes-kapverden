We use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The fonts are stored on this website itself, so no data is sent to Google.

To deliver a consultation we also process your billing address, booking and payment details, and any information you choose to share for your case. We do this to fulfil the contract, issue invoices and keep our accounts (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

6. Sharing

We only share data when our service or the law requires it: with our accountant or tax advisor, payment providers, technical providers (hosting, email) and public authorities. Your data is never sold or passed on for advertising.

7. How long we keep data

  • Requests: up to 1 year after our last contact
  • Contracts and invoices: as long as legal retention rules require

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can withdraw any consent at any time. Just write to sun_vibesCV@proton.me.

9. Complaints

You can complain to a data protection authority: in Cabo Verde, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), Praia. If you live in the EU, you can also contact your national authority, for example the Datenschutzbehörde in Austria (Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna) or your federal state's authority in Germany.

10. Applicable law

We follow Cabo Verde's data protection law (Lei n.º 133/V/2001, as amended) and, where we offer our services to people in the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
